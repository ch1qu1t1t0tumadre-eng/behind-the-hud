[intrigued] The Legend of Zelda started with a kid who was afraid of a cave. And it's real. Shigeru Miyamoto grew up in Sonobe, a small town near Kyoto, in the nineteen fifties. As a boy, Miyamoto explored the hills, forests and lakes around his home. One day he found a dark cave. For days, he was too nervous to go in. Then he came back with a lantern and stepped inside. [dramatic] In other words, gaming's greatest adventure series began with one scared kid and a lamp. Miyamoto wanted players to feel that same mix of fear and wonder. So in 1986, The Legend of Zelda dropped you into an open world full of caves and secrets. Today, that real cave is reportedly blocked off. But its story lives on, because
