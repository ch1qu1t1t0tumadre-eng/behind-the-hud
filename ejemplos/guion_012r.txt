[intrigued] Zelda exists because a kid was scared of a cave. Shigeru Miyamoto grew up near Kyoto in the nineteen fifties. As a boy, Miyamoto explored the hills, forests and lakes around his home. One day he found a dark cave. For days, he was too nervous to go in. Then he came back with a lantern and stepped inside. [dramatic] In other words, gaming's greatest adventure began with one scared kid and a lamp. Miyamoto wanted players to feel that same fear and wonder.
