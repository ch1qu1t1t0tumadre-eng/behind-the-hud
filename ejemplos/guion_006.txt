In 1986, Kazuhisa Hashimoto was porting the arcade shooter Gradius to Nintendo's home console. But there was a problem. Gradius was brutally hard, and Hashimoto couldn't beat it while testing. So he hid a secret button combo. Up, up, down, down, left, right, left, right, B, A. Enter the Konami Code, and your ship got every power-up. [dramatic] In other words, the developer cheated at his own game. He meant to delete the Konami Code before release. It shipped anyway.
